How it works and how you pay

The same engine answers on this website and inside Sokosumi. You see the price before anything happens.

  1. Choosepick the service for your role and country
  2. See the pricefixed before you pay, in test USDM on Cardano preprod
  3. Funds are lockedin a Masumi smart contract, not sent to us
  4. Result is deliveredthe hash of the report is written on-chain
  5. Seller is paidafter the dispute window; refund rules apply if we fail

We show four separate facts: report ready, funds locked, result hash confirmed on-chain, seller paid out. A hash proves a report is unchanged; it does not prove the data is true. A correct negative report is a completed service.

Germany and India - and what we do not claim

Germany

Open ALKIS fragment (Magdeburg, Sachsen-Anhalt) with motorway corridor screening, live checks on BKG, OpenStreetMap, BfN, PVGIS and Open-Meteo, German NDA and pooling documents in German. Prices in EUR where money amounts appear in leases.

India

Audits for Gujarat and Andhra Pradesh with state knowledge (other states get a site-wide condition), lease checks in INR per acre, English NDA, Land Pooling Agreement and grid-connectivity drafts. Protected-area and habitation screens rely on OpenStreetMap completeness, which we have not measured.

Not promised: ownership verification, planning permission, or grid-connection availability. Unavailable sources show up as limitations, never as a pass. Demonstration parcels are labelled as such.
